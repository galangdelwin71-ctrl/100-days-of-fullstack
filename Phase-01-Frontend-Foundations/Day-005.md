---

## 🔍 3. Line-by-Line Code Breakdown
- `<ul>`: Unordered list (renders bullet points by default).
- `<ol>`: Ordered list (renders numbers 1, 2, 3 automatically).
- `<li>`: List item element (must be a direct child of ul or ol).
