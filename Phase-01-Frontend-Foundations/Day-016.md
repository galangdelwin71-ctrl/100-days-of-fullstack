## 🔍 3. Line-by-Line Code Breakdown
- `flex-wrap: wrap`: Essential for responsive design; children wrap onto multiple lines instead of shrinking to unreadable sizes.
- `flex-grow: 1`: Forces child item to stretch and fill all remaining vacant space in row.
